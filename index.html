    Driver’s manual

    BrakeJumpGas

    Hold Gas and Brake in the bottom corners, and press Jump to hop. Tap them on a phone or click them with the mouse on a PC. At a door, press the gold Enter button.

    • →DGas
    • ←ABrake
    • Space↑Jump
    • EEnter a door

    Pro tips

    • Airborne? Gas lifts the nose and brake drops it, so you can line up your landing.
    • Steep climb? Ease off the gas so the front wheels stay down.
    • Haunted houses: bats and ghosts mean a wrong door. Enter one and you're spooked, and the run is over.
    • Ghosts: ghosts float over every haunted house, and now and then one stands at the door. If any ghost touches your car, you're spooked and the run is over, so don't jump into the roof ghosts, and wait while a ghost is at the door.
    • Hop it: jump over crates and cans, or to clear a bump at speed.
